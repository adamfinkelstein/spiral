1. Low resolution (1024², ×5.42): spacing, center, phase

a. Input: the card at low resolution, masked to the marked annulus

The full-res height map averaged over each low-res pixel (2×2 samples per pixel, the grain averaged over 6×6), times a soft mask from the hand-marked circles (dashed; ramps over one spacing inside them), minus its mean and scaled to unit rms. An idealized card: flat, no warp, bend, dust or scratches; only grain. Click any card view to pick the detail spot (red box).

White + hand mark, cyan low-res fit, yellow full-res refit, red + truth.

b. FFT of a × weight (central half of log |FFT|)

The image is weighted about the hand mark so the outer turns don't dominate, then transformed. The spiral's rings sit at radius N/spacing.

Dashed: the search window. Cyan: the peak found.

c. Radial profile → spacing

Mean log |FFT| per radius (smoothed by 1 bin), labelled by spacing in full-res px. The spacing is the peak within the window around the machine's nominal spacing, refined parabolically; more than 3% from the nominal → rejected, nominal used. No harmonics: the groove is nearly a cosine.

Grey band: the window; cyan: peak; white dashed: nominal; red: true spacing.

d. Center search: spiral coherence

|Z| = |Σ v·e−i(r/b − α)| for candidate centers on a grid about the hand mark, at the FFT spacing: the depth is ≈ −cos(r/b − α + φ) about the true center, so |Z| peaks there and arg Z is the phase. The lobe is only ~0.4 spacing wide, narrower than the hand mark's error, hence the grid; then a pattern search over x, y and spacing.

White + hand mark (grid center), cyan: answer, red +: truth.

e. Gradient-line center (comparison only)

The Part 1 method: lines along the gradients of the tangentially blurred image, RANSAC for the point most of them pass through (with the spiral's sideways correction). Not used downstream.

(~2 s)

Green: lines within the tolerance of its answer (magenta ring), red: the rest (drawn first); red +: truth.

2. Full resolution: unrolled strip, refit, groove trace

f. Detail at full resolution

The full-res depth around the spot (click to move). Red: traced groove (magenta where on the wrong groove), green: true centerline.

g. Strip offsets and the geometry refit

The strip runs along the low-res spiral: a column every few px of groove, each a radial line sampled at ±1 spacing, detrended and demodulated: Z = Σ v·e−2πid/S (cos² window) gives the groove offset o = −S·arg(−Z)/2π (mod S) and a confidence |Z|.

Dots: one per 40 columns, inner turns left, smoothed over σ 24 px for display (brightness ∝ |Z|). Relative to the strip's spiral, a center error is a one-turn wave, a spacing error a slope, a phase error an offset. The refit (yellow) fits exactly that, modulo S: a grid over spacing and center, then robust least squares. Red: the same for the true center, spacing and phase (no drift) — yellow should follow it. Green: the true grooves.

h. Unrolled strip around the spot

Columns along the groove, rows across it (1 px, outward up); the middle row is the low-res spiral; each column's mean is removed for display. Yellow: refitted spiral (±S faint); red: trace; green: true groove.

i. The trace: groove offset from the refitted spiral

Each column's Z is smoothed along the groove (the sideways path is smooth), and the trace takes the groove nearest a reference: the refitted spiral plus the slowly varying residual (within ±S/2 of the spiral), so noise can't move it to another turn.

Red: traced; green: true (drift and wobble, as seen from the fitted spiral).

j. Trace error per turn

Bars: RMS distance from the traced point to the nearest true groove centerline (dotted: S/4). Red: share of the turn on the wrong groove (0–100% of the height).

3. Sound readout (hill-and-dale: the depth of the groove along the trace)

k. Depth at the groove bottom

The sound is the depth of the cut (hill-and-dale): the stylus moves up and down, and where it rises above the card the groove disappears. Read from the traced centerline: the land at ±S/2 minus the bottom (each a mean over ±w), relative to its own average over 300 px of groove, minus 1. A gap reads −1 (the floor).

Red: read out; green: the true sound of the traced groove (relative depth, floored at −1 where nothing is cut).
