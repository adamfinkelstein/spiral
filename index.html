Spiral experiments

Interactive experiments on images of an Archimedean spiral (or concentric rings). Source: github.com/adamfinkelstein/spiral

Ring spacing from the FFT The spiral and the log magnitude of its 2D Fourier transform, with a radial profile and a harmonic-sum estimate of the ring spacing; then the center and phase from the spiral coherence map (the card pipeline's method, live in the browser). Center and phase from chunks The full spiral next to only a few random pieces of it, for exploring how well the center and phase can be recovered from partial data, given the ring spacing. An idealized card, whole pipeline A simulated recording card (40 turns, 29 px groove spacing and paper grain from the sonorine scans; the sound cut into the groove's depth). From a hand-marked center to the traced groove and its sound, every step shown and scored against the truth over many runs. Built in Python; the earlier in-browser version is card_js.html.

The methods, step by step: Procedure (Part 1: spacing, Part 2: center and phase, Part 3: an idealized card).
