Spiral experiments

Interactive experiments on images of an Archimedean spiral (or concentric rings). Source: github.com/adamfinkelstein/spiral

Ring spacing from the FFT The spiral and the log magnitude of its 2D Fourier transform, with a radial profile and a harmonic-sum estimate of the ring spacing. Center and phase from chunks The full spiral next to only a few random pieces of it, for exploring how well the center and phase can be recovered from partial data, given the ring spacing.

The methods, step by step: Procedure (Part 1: spacing, Part 2: center and phase).
