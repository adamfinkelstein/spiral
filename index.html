Spiral experiments

Interactive experiments on images of an Archimedean spiral (or concentric rings). Source: github.com/adamfinkelstein/spiral

Ring spacing from the FFT The spiral, masked by a simulated hand mark, and the log magnitude of its 2D Fourier transform; the ring spacing is the peak of the radial profile, then the center and phase come from the spiral coherence map: the card pipeline's low-res stage, live in the browser. Center and phase from chunks The full spiral next to only a few random pieces of it, for exploring how well the center and phase can be recovered from partial data, given the ring spacing. An idealized card, whole pipeline A simulated recording card (40 turns, 29 px groove spacing and paper grain from the sonorine scans; the sound cut into the groove's depth). From a hand-marked center to the traced groove and its sound, every step shown and scored against the truth over many runs. Built in Python; the earlier in-browser version is card_js.html.

The method, step by step: Procedure (steps 0–6, from the scanned card to its sound). Everything tried and dropped, and the methods of the spacing and center pages: Considered.
