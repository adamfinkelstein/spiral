Spiral experiments

Interactive experiments on images of an Archimedean spiral (or concentric rings). Source: github.com/adamfinkelstein/spiral

Every page is in the menu at the top right.

Procedure The method, step by step: steps 0–6, from the scanned card to its sound.
Simulated card: results A simulated recording card (40 turns, 29 px groove spacing and paper grain from the sonorine scans; the sound cut into the groove's depth) run through every step of the procedure, scored against the truth over many cards. Built in Python.
Considered and dropped Everything tried and dropped, and the methods of the spacing and center pages.
Ring spacing (FFT) The spiral, masked by a simulated hand mark, and the log magnitude of its 2D Fourier transform; the ring spacing is the peak of the radial profile, then the center and phase come from the spiral coherence map: the card pipeline's low-res stage, live in the browser. Its controls are explained on Ring spacing: the controls.
Center from chunks The full spiral next to only a few random pieces of it, for exploring how well the center and phase can be recovered from partial data, given the ring spacing.
Card in the browser (frozen) The earlier in-browser version of the simulated card, kept as it was.
