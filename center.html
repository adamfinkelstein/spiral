Chunks

Center: — drag in the left image to move it (hold Shift for 10× finer). Arrow keys nudge 1 px (Shift: 0.1 px). Double-click to recenter.

Each chunk is a piece of the curve of the given arc length, placed uniformly at random along the whole spiral (or along all the rings together). The chunks stay put as you change the other controls; New chunks draws a new random set ().
